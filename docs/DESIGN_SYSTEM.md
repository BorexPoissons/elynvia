# ELYNVIA — Design System Direction v0.1

Status: visual direction only; detailed UI system comes later.

## Brand character

ELYNVIA should feel:

- intelligent;
- human;
- calm;
- premium;
- trustworthy;
- powerful without feeling dystopian;
- simple enough for non-technical users.

## Product UI principle

Complex intelligence should produce a simple interface.

The Life home experience should initially center on the user’s intention rather than navigation complexity.

## Visual direction

Early exploration may use deep neutral/dark surfaces with luminous blue/cyan/violet accents and restrained success indicators. These are not yet fixed brand tokens.

Avoid visual clichés that make ELYNVIA look like a generic AI chatbot, crypto product or science-fiction control panel.

## Accessibility

Final design tokens must support adequate contrast, keyboard navigation, visible focus states, reduced-motion preferences and readable typography.

## Internationalization

Layouts should anticipate multilingual text expansion and locale-specific date, time, number and currency formats.

Detailed typography, colors, spacing, logo rules and component tokens will be established during the Life UI milestone.
